Watch Party

Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Watch Party does not collect personal information. It keeps several people's video players in step while each of them watches the same film on their own streaming account. It does not stream video, and it never reads, records or forwards the video itself.

What leaves your browser

While you are in a room, and only then, the extension sends the events that keep the players together: play, pause, seek, the current position, the playback rate, an identifier of the film taken from the address of the page, the six-character room code, an anonymous member id the extension generates for itself, and the display name you choose. Chat messages are passed through the same connection. That is the whole list.

Nothing is sent when you are not in a room.

What never leaves your browser

Page content, cookies, browsing history, your account on any streaming service, your email, your login, and any identifier of you as a person. The extension has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and makes no third-party request of any kind.

Where it goes, and for how long

The room events and chat messages travel through our relay server, which passes them to the other people in the room and keeps nothing: they are not written to a database, to a log or to a disk. When the last person leaves, the room stops existing. Closing the room ends the conversation with it.

What is stored on your computer

Using chrome.storage.local, which stays on this computer and is never synchronised to a Google account: the anonymous member id, your display name, whether you have seen the introduction, the "follow the host" switch, the last room code, and the counters that decide when the rating question may be shown. Removing the extension removes all of it.

Permissions, and why each one is needed

  • storage — to keep the settings listed above on this computer.
  • offscreen — to hold the room connection in a hidden document, because Chrome stops an extension's background worker after about 30 seconds of quiet and the room would drop with it.
  • sidePanel — to show the room's text chat in Chrome's own side panel.
  • contextMenus — to add "Start a watch party here" and "Copy invite link" to the right-click menu.
  • tabs — to find the tab that is playing the film, and the invite tab a guest opened, so that clicking a link is enough to get into the room.
  • scripting — to insert the synchronisation code into the film's tab, on the site you granted and on no other.

Websites the extension can reach

  • https://alxrum.github.io/watch-party-pages/j/* — our own invite page. It is how an invite link hands the room code to the extension.
  • https://www.netflix.com/* and https://www.youtube.com/* — the two sites the extension is built for, so that clicking a friend's link is the whole of joining. Chrome lists them when you install it. The access is used for one thing: reading and setting the position of that page's player. Nothing on those pages is read, stored or sent anywhere else.
  • https://*/* — optional, and never requested for every website at once: Chrome asks for the single site you are on, and only if you start a party on a player we have not named. You can withdraw it in chrome://extensions at any time, and the extension does nothing on a site you have not granted.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
